# ClaimShield AI — Short Hackathon Pitch

Healthcare claim fraud is rarely just one suspicious record. It can hide across repeated providers, patients, billing patterns and connected claims.

ClaimShield AI is a human-friendly fraud intelligence platform. A normal user can screen a claim in plain language, get a 0–100 review-priority score, and understand exactly why the claim looks unusual.

What makes us different is what happens next. Fraud Network Intelligence connects suspicious claims, patients and providers to reveal possible organized patterns. Provider Risk Passports build a living trust profile over time. Human-in-the-loop feedback captures investigator decisions so a future production model can continuously improve.

Our hackathon version is frontend-only for a reliable demo, but the product is designed to scale into supervised ML, anomaly detection, graph analytics and explainable AI.

ClaimShield AI does not replace investigators. It gives them a clearer starting point, a wider view of connected fraud, and a faster path to the claims that matter most.
